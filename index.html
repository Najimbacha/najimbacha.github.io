01 / MOBILE APP

Wazn.

Wazn brings meal tracking into focus. Take a photo of your food to get AI-powered estimates of calories, protein, carbohydrates, and fat—making it easier to keep a food diary without searching for every ingredient.

Beyond the camera, Wazn brings together barcode scanning, personal calorie and macro targets, and a daily nutrition dashboard. Water, weight, and activity tracking help you follow your routine, while an AI nutrition coach offers another way to explore food-related questions. It’s a practical companion for paying closer attention to everyday habits.

Saudi Driving Theory Test

Preparing for your Saudi driving theory test should start with understanding. This independent learning app offers practice in English, Arabic, Urdu, and Hindi, helping learners study in the language they feel most comfortable using.

With more than 600 practice questions, 25 timed mock exams, and a road-sign library, it supports everything from your first study session to a final review. Guided lessons and flashcards help you build knowledge step by step. Answer explanations, saved mistakes, and progress breakdowns show you where to focus next—and offline access lets you keep practising wherever you are.

ABOUT

Curious mind.
Hands-on builder.

I’m Najim Bacha, a Flutter developer, electrical engineer, and founder of Mindora Lab, based in Taif, Saudi Arabia. I build practical mobile apps and work with the electrical systems that support everyday life.

My projects reflect that same practical approach. Wazn makes food tracking easier to fit into a busy day, while my Saudi driving theory app helps learners prepare in a language they understand. I enjoy taking a complicated task and making it feel simpler.

Through Mindora Lab, my social media agency, I also explore a different kind of building: turning stories from the ancient world into short videos for today’s audiences. Technology, engineering, and storytelling give me different ways to turn an idea into something people can use, understand, or enjoy.
